Florida undergrounding research

Primary sources already cited on this site. Each line is the publisher’s document, not a Walton finding.

  • Storm Secure Underground Program — Florida Power & Light. How FPL describes neighborhood laterals, vegetation outages, easements, and its own 2024 comparison of FPL lines.
  • 2024 Storm Protection Plan annual status report — Florida Power & Light, filed with the Florida Public Service Commission. Voluntary municipal undergrounding agreements in 2024, separate from the storm-plan program.
  • Undergrounding to Reduce Florida Power System Vulnerability to Extreme Weather (June 2024) — U.S. Department of Energy and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. Duke Energy Florida’s targeted program, and the 2005 statewide cost context. Duke does not serve Walton County.
  • Staff audit of Duke Energy Florida’s storm protection programs — Florida Public Service Commission. Treats flood hardening of underground equipment as its own program.
  • Section 366.96, Florida Statutes — Florida Legislature. Storm protection plans and cost recovery for investor-owned electric utilities.
  • Staff analysis of the 2019 storm-protection bill — Florida House of Representatives. How approved undergrounding in a storm plan can be recovered from customers generally.
  • Rule 25-6.115 — Florida Administrative Code. Charges when an applicant asks an investor-owned utility to convert existing overhead distribution.
  • Rule 25-6.078 — Florida Administrative Code. Underground service in new residential subdivisions, which is a different case from converting an existing road.
  • Section 125.01, Florida Statutes — Florida Legislature. County authority to create a municipal service taxing unit or benefit unit.
  • 2025 Local Government Financial Information Handbook — Florida Office of Economic and Demographic Research. Describes an MSTU as the tax mechanism and an MSBU as charges or assessments.
  • Offices and service area — Choctawhatchee Electric Cooperative. The cooperative’s statement that it serves most of Walton County. Not an undergrounding program.
  • Form 8-K, December 18, 2020 — NextEra Energy, filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Gulf Power merged into FPL effective January 1, 2021.
  • News release, January 1, 2022 — Florida Power & Light. Northwest Florida service under the unified FPL name.

Other community examples

These are not direct models for Walton County. The longer write-ups are on Examples.

  • Town of Palm Beach undergrounding project — Town of Palm Beach. The town’s own site for a phased, town-wide conversion.
  • Phase 8 described as the last phase — Town of Palm Beach. A town calendar entry, not a Walton schedule.
  • Project note, week of September 28, 2026 — Town of Palm Beach. Drilling, conduit, street restoration, and pole removal still underway.
  • January 2018 town update (PDF) — Town of Palm Beach. Opening financing for the initial phases. Not a total project cost.
  • Undergrounding programs description — California Public Utilities Commission. What underground facilities include, and why buried faults can take longer to find.
  • Electric Rule 20 — California Public Utilities Commission. How cities requested conversions, and how the funding rules were built.
  • Decision 21-06-013 (June 3, 2021) — California Public Utilities Commission. Stopped new Rule 20A work-credit allocations.
  • Decision 23-06-008 (June 8, 2023) — California Public Utilities Commission. Discontinued Rule 20A going forward.
  • News release, May 6, 2025 — CenterPoint Energy. The company’s report of 400 miles undergrounded in Greater Houston.
  • Greater Houston Resiliency Initiative summary — CenterPoint Energy. A later company progress summary. Not an audited Walton figure.
  • Docket 57579 stipulation and settlement, June 12, 2025 — Public Utility Commission of Texas. The settlement’s description of strategic undergrounding and its cost estimate.
  • Open-meeting minutes, November 14, 2025 — Public Utility Commission of Texas. The commission adopted a final order in that docket.
