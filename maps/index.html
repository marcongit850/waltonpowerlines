Understanding the suggested study areas

This drawing is orientation only — not a proposed construction map. It is not to scale, not a survey, and not a map of poles.

What a real feasibility study could map

Who owns the lines

Electric, cable, and phone can share a pole. CHELCO likely serves essentially all of Scenic Highway 30A. Miramar Beach, west of that corridor, is largely FPL. This drawing does not show ownership. A feasibility study would confirm it.

Overhead and underground

A study could mark which segments are still overhead and which are already underground. Those segments are not drawn here.

Constraints

Drainage, trees, driveways, bridges, and pavement condition change what work is even possible. None of that is on this sketch.

Potential segments

A study could suggest shorter pieces of a possible corridor for a closer look. Suggesting a segment would still not approve construction.

These detailed maps do not exist yet. Nothing on this page is a county GIS layer, a utility ownership map, or a construction plan.
