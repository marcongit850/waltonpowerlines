A note of interest, not a vote

If you live, work, or own property in Walton County, you can say so. Checking the study box means you would like a feasibility study. It does not sign a petition, set a tax, or tell the county commission how to vote.

Send a note

Say what community you are in, and whether your interest is reliability, the look of the road, or both. Specific outage stories help a future study ask the utilities better questions. They are not, by themselves, a construction request.

What this note is. A message through the form on this page. Checking the study box is not a petition, not a vote, and not a pledge of money.
